Jamie Lakritz is an award-winning British playwright whose work ranges from acclaimed festival dramas to crowd-pleasing Christmas comedies. His published plays include *The Start of Something*, *Of All the Things I Have Lost*, *Songkeeper*, and *The Great Christmas Cracker Heist*.

Jamie's writing blends sharp dialogue, theatrical invention, and a wry sense of humour. His plays have been performed by groups across the world, as well as by his own company, [Brightlight Theatre](https://brightlight-theatre.co.uk), which he founded to develop and stage new writing.

A regular winner of *Best New Play* at the Woking Drama Festival, his debut play *The Start of Something* also received the Derek Jacobi Playwriting Award in 2017, and was later performed at Theatre503, directed by veteran actor Sam Cox and starring *BBC Coupling*'s Kate Isitt.

Jamie trained in playwriting at the National Theatre under Ryan Craig, was a member of Director's Cut Theatre's Writer's Club (2021) and Broken Silence Theatre's Playwright Hive (2022). He continues to write and direct for Brightlight Theatre.
